# Product Brief: Movie Dashboard

## Executive Summary

Movie Dashboard is a small client-side library of Studio Ghibli films. Its primary purpose is to help the project owner practice the complete BMad workflow and learn enough from each phase to repeat it on other projects. Friends are the secondary audience, browsing film information in their free time.

## Product Experience

The Home route fetches and presents the Studio Ghibli film list for browsing. Friends can search the fetched list and open a film's details. The app also has Contact and About routes.

The full client stack, interaction details, and API contract reference are in [addendum.md](addendum.md).

## Success Criteria

- The project owner completes each BMad step and phase with enough understanding to repeat the process on another project.
- Friends can browse the Studio Ghibli film list and inspect individual film details.

## Scope and Direction

Movie playback is out of scope. Visual design will be decided in a separate UX discussion. The project is expected to continue evolving through future features and maintenance, but no later features are defined yet.
